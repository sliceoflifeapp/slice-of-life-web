What's new

Every update, in plain English.

v0.5.1 August 24, 2026 Latest
  • Twice as much footage in one Slice. You can now drop in up to 300 clips, double the previous limit — useful for a long day of filming or a whole trip at once.
  • Vertical Slices now fill the frame for HDR footage, which is most video shot on an iPhone. Those clips were still falling back to blurred bars.
  • When a Slice is too big to build, the message now tells you whether it was the number of clips or the amount of footage.
v0.5.0 August 22, 2026
  • Undo and redo. Arrows at the top left of the review screen step back through anything you change — moving a clip, hiding one, rotating, captions, music, effects. Reset puts the whole screen back to the cut Slice suggested, and even that is undoable.
  • Make the clips bigger. A slider at the top right scales the thumbnails up, so you can tell one shot from another at a glance.
  • Save a Slice to finish later. “Save for later” keeps your arrangement without rendering it. The Slice waits on your home screen with a green DRAFT badge — open it and every edit is exactly where you left it. Building it later costs nothing extra.
  • Watch the walkthrough without leaving the app. The play button in the title bar opens the full tour of how Slice works.
  • Dragging a clip up the list is easier — the list now scrolls on its own when you drag near the top or bottom.
  • Photos always play as their own moment now, instead of sometimes being tucked under narration as a cutaway.
  • Fixed a clip losing a rotation you had set by hand when you pressed Reset.
  • Fixed the review screen bringing back edits from last time when you started a fresh Slice — that only happens now when you deliberately reopen one.
v0.4.4 August 20, 2026
  • No more mode picker. Drop in your footage and go. If there's no talking to camera, Slice offers a b-roll montage instead.
  • Narrate a b-roll montage. Hit the mic, record, and your voice is mixed over it — captions included.
  • Bigger Build button, sharper text, and a fix for b-roll-only Slices losing color grading and 10-bit export.
v0.4.3 August 19, 2026
  • The iPhone companion is on the App Store. Send clips straight from your phone to your Mac and have a Slice waiting for you. The iPhone Sync setting now links the listing.
  • Fixed the walkthrough appearing on top of the license screen the very first time you opened the app, where it covered the box you were typing into.
  • Fixed the home tour skipping over your recent projects — it now always shows that step.
  • The filming guide no longer opens by itself after the tour. It's still there behind the document icon whenever you want it.
  • Fixed "Start over" in the review screen leaving a speed change on a clip: the badge disappeared but the clip still rendered sped up.
v0.4.2 August 17, 2026
  • Take your Slice to any editor. The done screen's Export XML button now offers Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, and DaVinci Resolve — each with its own import steps. Final Cut support is brand new, and DaVinci Resolve is back: the footage-compatibility problems that made us pull it (clips importing offline or frozen) are properly fixed.
  • The export now matches your edit exactly. Rotation — automatic and manual — carries into all three editors for the first time, along with speed changes and word-level edit cuts. A voiceover recorded over a b-roll montage lands on its own audio track as the raw recording, so you can balance it yourself.
  • Tighter narration. Talking clips now trim right to where you start and stop speaking, so videos no longer hang on a second or two of silence at either end of a narration clip — in the video and in the exports.
  • Reopening an older Slice picks up the improved trims automatically, without re-analyzing your footage.
  • Fixed clips that turned mid-recording importing sideways in Final Cut and DaVinci Resolve.
v0.4.1 August 16, 2026
  • Add clips you forgot, without starting over. There's a new + button in the review screen. Pick the clips or photos you missed and they're added to the Slice you're already working on — only the new ones get analyzed, your arrangement stays exactly as you left it, and it costs no extra credits. Added clips arrive together as their own b-roll montage at the point in the day they belong to, so nothing you've already arranged gets moved around.
  • Set aside a narration clip, not just b-roll. Clicking the × on a section's narration clip now tucks it into the Hidden clips list like any other clip, and restoring it brings it back as narration in its original spot.
  • Fixed a restored clip reappearing in the review screen but still being left out of the finished video.
  • Fixed caption and word-level edits getting lost when a clip was switched between narration and b-roll.
  • Fixed Shorts not being made when you reopened a Slice and rebuilt it.
  • Small things: the text-based editing button now lights up when it's holding edits, the same way the caption button does.
v0.4.0 August 15, 2026
  • Pick up a finished Slice and keep editing. Every video you've made now has an Edit button, and your recent Slices sit on the home screen. Click one and it opens right back in the review screen, arranged exactly the way you built it. Change anything and rebuild. It's free and it doesn't re-analyze your footage, so it's quick.
  • Your recent Slices, on the home screen. A grid of your latest videos replaces the old scrolling strip. Click one to keep editing it, or hit play to just watch it.
  • Speed up a clip. Right-click any b-roll clip and pick 2x, 4x, 8x or 20x to turn a long take into a quick time-lapse. Sped-up clips are silent, and the speed carries through to the Premiere XML too.
  • Better vertical framing. The crop that follows your face in vertical exports now works on clips where you turned the phone mid-recording.
  • Cutaways no longer open on the moment you were still setting the camera down — most noticeable on sped-up clips, which start much further into the shot now.
  • Fixed reopening a video quietly changing its pacing, which could drop a b-roll clip or stretch the gaps between sections.
  • Small things: clearer Edit and Watch buttons on the done screen, a bigger narration marker in the review screen, and two icons that weren't showing up at all.
v0.3.0 August 12, 2026
  • Turned your phone mid-shot? Fixed automatically. iPhone clips where you started recording one way and rotated to another (vertical to horizontal, upside down to right side up) now come out the right way up on their own. This was the most common cause of sideways or upside-down clips.
  • Voiceover on montages. Every b-roll montage in the review screen now has a mic button — record a narration right onto it, and the montage's own sound ducks under your voice. Captions included if you have them on.
  • Resizable window. Drag any corner — everything scales together.
  • Rotate left. Right-clicking a clip now offers both directions, and the little turn animation always spins the way you clicked.
  • Fixed two rotation bugs with talking-to-camera clips: some rendered upside down, and manually rotating them in the review screen didn't stick.
  • Fixed caption edits being lost if you closed the caption editor before building.
v0.2.6 August 9, 2026
  • Text-based editing. Open a section in the review screen and you'll see what you said as plain text. Click a word to cut it, or drag across a few to cut a phrase — it removes that bit from the video and the audio, not just the caption. Pauses show up as their own marker with how long they are, so you can drop the ones you don't want.
  • Cut quality. Flip this on and each word is shaded by how cleanly a cut there will land — green where there's a real pause to hide it in, red where you're mid-sentence and it may sound abrupt. Cuts snap to the quietest moment nearby on their own.
  • Your text edits are remembered if you come back to the same footage later.
  • More Shorts per vlog. Some renders were producing far fewer than they should have.
v0.2.5 August 8, 2026
  • Guided tours. The home screen and the review screen now walk you through themselves one step at a time, highlighting each control as it explains it. They run once automatically, and you can replay either from the ? icon whenever you want.
  • Trimmed the filming guide down to the essentials.

  • Fixed a hidden clip occasionally coming back into a new b-roll montage instead of returning to its original section.

  • Music placement now tracks changes in video length after a Re-edit (e.g. switching pacing), instead of cutting off early.
  • The captions toggle no longer shows up in B-roll mode, where it never applied.
  • A batch of smaller reliability fixes found while testing all of the above.
