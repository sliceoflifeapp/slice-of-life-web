Who this works for

We only take on work the software and the process are actually good at, so it's worth being straight about the shape of it up front.

  • You talk to camera. This is built for vlogs — a spoken thread with b-roll cut around it. Not event coverage, walkthroughs, or footage with no narration.
  • You publish at least weekly. Below that a monthly retainer doesn't make sense for you, and we'd rather say so now than after you've paid for one.
  • Up to 300 clips or three hours of source per video. One day or a whole trip — whatever you shot, as long as it's going into one video. Mixed cameras are fine: phone, action cam, drone, all in one folder.

What happens next

Four steps, and you can stop at any of them.

  1. 1 You send a link One video you've already published, plus roughly how often you post. That's the whole application.
  2. 2 We tell you straight, within a day Whether your footage will work well, and what we'd do with it. If it isn't a fit we'll say so rather than take the money.
  3. 3 You send your footage. We edit it, free One video's worth. Finished video, captions and vertical Shorts — the same thing a paying client gets, with your own branding on the graphics.
  4. 4 You decide Like it, pick a plan and we start. Don't, and nothing's owed — keep the video either way.
