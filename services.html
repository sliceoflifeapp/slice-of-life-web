Our process

How your vlog gets made

Most editing services get faster by hiring more editors. We got faster by writing the software. You drop the whole day into a private folder we set up for you — no renaming, no sorting, nothing to install. Here's what happens next.

STEP 01

Our proprietary software does the heavy lifting

Slice of Life — our own Mac app — watches the whole day. It finds your talking-to-camera moments, matches cutaways to what you're saying, trims dead air and burns in captions. The mechanical half of the edit, done before a person touches it.

See the software →

STEP 02

Then a person actually edits it

A first pass isn't a finished video. Someone has to find the story — which take carries it, what's funny, what to cut even though the footage is good. That's taste, and no tool has it.

STEP 03

You review, then it's yours

One link, within 24 hours. Comment on anything you want changed and we re-cut — usually the same day. Then it lands ready to upload: the finished video, your vertical Shorts and the caption file. Nothing left for you to do.

The work

We built our own editing software. It handles the slow mechanical half of a vlog edit — finding your talking-to-camera moments, matching b-roll, trimming dead air, burning captions — in minutes rather than hours. That leaves the day for the judgment calls a person actually has to make. Turnaround runs on business days, so footage sent on a Friday comes back Monday.
Vlogs. Daily vlogs, travel vlogs, and anything else where you talk to camera and b-roll shows what you're describing. It's a narrow focus on purpose — it's what the software was built for, and it's what we're good at.

Working together

We set up a private upload folder for you on day one. Drag the whole card in exactly as it came off the camera — no file naming, no folder structure, no organizing. It's the same place your finished videos come back to.

A round is one set of notes, so send everything you want changed together rather than a message at a time. Swapping a take, moving a cut, fixing a caption — all revisions. Re-cutting the video around a different story is a new video.
